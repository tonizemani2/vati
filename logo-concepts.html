Ten marks for a forecasting research lab

Each concept leans on the thesis spine: rent accrues to the binding constraint, and the edge is spotting where it moves before it's priced. So the recurring motifs are the dependency graph, the pinch point where a flow stops being elastic, the forecast cone, and the sealed, dated record. Every mark is shown large, in a wordmark lockup, and at favicon size on dark / light / brand. All single-color so they survive a 16px favicon and an embossed PDF seal.
