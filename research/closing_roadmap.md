# Closing roadmap — who, for what exactly, and in what order

*2026-06-17. Pairs with PEOPLE_MAP.md (the why) and outreach_messages.md (one bespoke message per
person). This file answers: for each target, what am I actually trying to close, what do I offer,
and when do I approach. Order is a default, not a law. If a warm intro opens early, take it.*

## What "closing" means here (the goal varies by person)

| Close type | The ask | What you give |
|---|---|---|
| **ADVISOR-ANCHOR** | the marquee forecasting name that makes the bench real | 0.5-1% equity, 2yr vest, light scope |
| **ADVISOR-TECH** | validates "real AI, not a prompt wrapper" | 0.25-0.5% equity |
| **ADVISOR-GTM** | opens fund / trading-desk client doors | 0.5-1% equity |
| **ADVISOR+CAPITAL** | advisor who also writes or sources a check | equity + the Ring-3 conversation |
| **AMPLIFIER** | a feature / quote / boost to their audience | a relationship + a great story, usually no equity |
| **VALIDATOR-ACADEMIC** | a name on the page for investor diligence | small equity, warm intro first |
| **COLLAB** | data / benchmark integration, mutual credibility | a relationship, attribution, no equity |
| **ASPIRATIONAL** | the field's biggest names, later | warm intro only, after polished proof |

## The order (phases, not a calendar straitjacket)

**Phase 0 — now to ~Jun 18 (prep, do not send).** Finish the ablation, lock the ForecastBench
upload, polish the public record page so the link carries the email. Map warm paths (who can intro
you to Tetlock, Silver, Mellers). Confirm the real identities behind pgodzinai and Panshul42 before
any equity ask. Nothing goes out cold without the proof attached.

**Phase 1 — Jun 19-22 (proof live, fire the peer cluster).** The people who convert hardest on a
fresh benchmark result and who personally answer "are you just a prompt layer": Karger, Halawi,
Liptay, Hickman, Sempere, Lifland, Wildeford, plus a verified top-bot peer. This is the cluster that
turns a cold founder into a credible one.

**Phase 2 — week of Jun 23 (broadcast + buyer side).** Amplifiers carry the win to the right rooms
(de Neufville, Gouker, Zvi, Munn, Chen). Quant advisors open the buyer door (Lebron, Hoffstein,
Hatch). Two-for-ones bridge to capital (Krug, Rajaprabhakaran).

**Phase 3 — once 1-2 advisors are signed (warm-path everything).** Use a signed advisor to reach
the academics (Mellers, Moore, Ungar, Steinhardt) and the marquee names (Tetlock via Rosenberg,
Aguirre, Silver). A warm intro here closes at 10-30x the cold rate. This is also when advisors start
feeding you Ring-2 pilots and Ring-3 angels.
