## Decision

Lead with P1, but revise the commercial wedge. "Power-secured AI campuses win"
is now too visible to sell as the core insight. DOE/LBNL, FERC, PJM, Blackstone,
Brookfield, KKR/Helix, hyperscalers, and major site-selection firms are already
talking about data-center power scarcity and time-to-power.

The sellable wedge is narrower and better:

> Verify or refute AI campus power claims before a buyer underwrites them.

A row is not `power_secured` until primary or official sources verify MW,
counterparty/offtake, interconnection or behind-the-meter path, and
permit/regulatory status. Otherwise it is `lead`, `source_verified`,
`contested`, or `unresolved_interconnection_risk`.

Keep P6 as the second offer lane: agent authority, auditability, and rollback
for CISOs and compliance buyers. If P1 does not produce a serious LOI or paid
sprint after the first 15 manual buyer notes plus 5 warm-path notes, demote P1
behind P6.

## First Geography

Start in PJM / Mid-Atlantic.

"we discovered the power bottleneck." The pitch is "we can prove whether the
claimed power path survives primary-source diligence."

## Stop Rules

Stop outreach to a segment if three calls in a row reveal no active asset,
budget owner, or timing decision.

Stop a dossier row if no primary/official source can verify the named MW,
counterparty, interconnection path, or permitting status after two focused
source passes.

Stop the thesis as a launch wedge if buyers repeatedly say they already have
better internal time-to-power intelligence and will not pay for outside
verification.
