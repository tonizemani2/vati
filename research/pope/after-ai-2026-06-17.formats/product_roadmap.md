# Product Roadmap - After AI: where the constraint moves when intelligence leaves the screen

## Phase 1: Artifact Product

- Produce sector Pope boards.
- Compile format packs automatically.
- Keep a sealed scorecard and watchlist.
- Use public benchmarks as credibility, not product definition.

## Phase 2: Monitoring Product

- Turn every watch signal into a tracked item.
- Add owner, cadence, source links, status, and last-checked fields.
- Alert when a watch signal moves or a kill condition appears.

## Phase 3: Buyer Workflow

- Map forecasts to capex, procurement, investment, compliance, and partnership decisions.
- Add buyer-specific dashboards and monthly decision memos.
- Let customers mark which decisions changed and what value was saved or created.

## Phase 4: Data Moat

- Build a structured constraint graph: domain, bottleneck, exposed party, metric, repricing channel, source, probability, resolution.
- Score every dated clause.
- Train or fine-tune on the internal trace once there is enough proprietary record.

## Phase 5: API / Platform

- Expose compact API summaries.
- Support sector feeds and watchlist exports.
- Add CRM hooks for design-partner and sales workflows.
