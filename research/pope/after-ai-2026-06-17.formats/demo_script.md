# 10-Minute Demo Script - After AI: where the constraint moves when intelligence leaves the screen

## 0:00 - 1:00 Frame

We do not ask whether AI is big. We ask where the bottleneck moves once everyone believes AI is big.

## 1:00 - 3:00 Show The Board

Use the PDF summary and open with P1: The AI frontier moves from model access to firm-power siting.

Say: the structural case is 82%, but the exact dated clause is 52%. That split is how we stay scoreable.

## 3:00 - 5:00 Show The Decision Layer

Buyer: Hyperscaler infrastructure teams, data-center developers, power developers, large AI labs with reserved compute needs, and investors underwriting AI infrastructure.

Decision changed: Data-center site selection, PPA strategy, power-development partnerships, capex phasing, and portfolio exposure to grid-dependent versus power-secured data-center assets.

Action now: Map sites by firm-power time-to-energize, not just land cost and fiber; secure options on campuses where geothermal, gas with carbon capture, nuclear restart, or other firm power can be contracted behind the meter.

## 5:00 - 7:00 Show The Watchlist

First signal: A hyperscaler or top data-center REIT announcing a 100 MW plus campus whose stated differentiator is behind-the-meter clean firm power rather than cheap land.

Kill condition: Kill if by end 2028 fewer than two hyperscaler-scale campuses publicly secure behind-the-meter firm clean generation as a core siting advantage, or if transformer and interconnection delays normalize below roughly 24 months in the main US AI data-center markets.

## 7:00 - 9:00 Show Format Flex

Open the investor one-pager, buyer action sheet, watchlist CSV, and scorecard JSONL. Same claim, different buyer surface, no claim drift.

## 9:00 - 10:00 Ask

Pick one live decision. We will run a sector-specific board and monitor the top watch signals for 30 days.
