## Slide 5 Candidate

P1 - The AI frontier moves from model access to firm-power siting.

- Structural / clause odds: 82% / 52%
- Buyer: Hyperscaler infrastructure teams, data-center developers, power developers, large AI labs with reserved compute needs, and investors underwriting AI infrastructure.
- Action: Map sites by firm-power time-to-energize, not just land cost and fiber; secure options on campuses where geothermal, gas with carbon capture, nuclear restart, or other firm power can be contracted behind the meter.
- Watch: A hyperscaler or top data-center REIT announcing a 100 MW plus campus whose stated differentiator is behind-the-meter clean firm power rather than cheap land.
