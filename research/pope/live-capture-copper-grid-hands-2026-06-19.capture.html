TC/RC inversion: the existing copper capture should be corrected from 'long smelter rent' to 'smelter distress plus policy-rescue optionality'.

PURSUE
Why capturable This is capturable now because it fixes a live error in the current board and turns that correction into a buyer-useful memo. Low or negative TC/RCs are not a clean signal that smelters own new rent. They are the fee smelters receive from miners, and they fall when concentrate supply is tight and smelters compete for feed. The right branch map is: (1) continued concentrate tightness crushes custom-smelter margins; (2) Western governments may rescue strategically important smelters because closures are hard to reverse; (3) if China capacity cuts and mine supply recover, TC/RCs rebound and the thesis changes again. A base-metals PM can trade that branch map immediately. An operator retainer is too slow. A paper-trade card plus a paid note is realistic.
