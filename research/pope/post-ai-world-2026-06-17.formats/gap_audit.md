# Gap Audit - The World After AI: intelligence gets permission to act

## What The Board Already Has

- Dated claims with probabilities, metrics, and kill conditions.
- Buyer-facing implications for each call.
- Renderable PDF/HTML and portable Markdown.
- Watchlist, scorecard, cards, sales copy, deck outline, and API summary.

## What It Still Needs

1. A first ICP wedge: one buyer segment to attack before broadening.
2. A paid offer ladder: what is free proof, what is design-partner paid, and what becomes enterprise/API.
3. A demo path: how to show value in under ten minutes without explaining the whole forecasting philosophy.
4. A monitoring runbook: who checks each watch signal and what happens when it moves.
5. A diligence FAQ: crisp answers to why this is better than expert networks, consultants, prediction markets, and analyst research.
6. A product roadmap: how Pope becomes a repeatable system with data feeds, alerts, scoring, and sector-specific boards.

## Recommended Next Move

Start with the buyer segment whose decision is most expensive and most time-sensitive. For this board, that is AI infrastructure and power-secured data-center siting. Use P1 as the flagship demo because the ROI story is easiest to understand: an energized site beats a cheaper stranded site.
