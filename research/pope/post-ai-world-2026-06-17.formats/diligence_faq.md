# Diligence FAQ - The World After AI: intelligence gets permission to act

## Is this just prediction markets?

No. Prediction markets price explicit questions. Pope names the upstream constraint before the market has a clean question or instrument for it.

## Is this just analyst research?

No. Analyst research usually explains sectors. Pope forces each call into a dated clause, kill condition, watch signal, and buyer decision.

## Is this just consulting?

No. Consulting sells bespoke judgment. Pope should become a repeatable system: structured calls, watchlists, scoring records, and reusable buyer formats.

## Why would anyone pay?

Because timing errors are expensive. The paid object is not a forecast paragraph; it is a decision update before procurement, capex, investment, policy, or compliance catches up.

## What is the moat?

A forward record of dated calls, structured constraint data, observed triggers, kill conditions, and sector-specific calibration. Benchmarks help open doors, but the moat is the live decision record.

## What if the exact clause is only 45 percent?

Good. The exact clause should be honest. A 45 percent call can still be valuable if consensus is lower or if the downside of ignoring it is large.

## Why use AI here?

AI gives breadth, adversarial pressure, format multiplication, and fast monitoring. The value comes from the workflow discipline: generate wide, gate hard, name the buyer, track the signal.
