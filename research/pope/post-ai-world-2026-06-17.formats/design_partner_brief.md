# Design Partner Brief - The World After AI: intelligence gets permission to act

## Ideal First Partner

A buyer with a live, expensive timing decision where a constraint signal changes action in the next 3 to 12 months.

## Recommended First Segment

Start with: agentic commerce / identity / payments.

Why: Early rails win standards gravity. If the agent economy turns into high-volume microtransactions, the infrastructure provider that owns credentialing and settlement gets transaction take-rate and compliance lock-in before apps commoditize.

## Pilot Promise

We will identify the next binding constraint, translate it into the decision it changes, and track the first observable signals that prove or kill the thesis.

## Pilot Deliverables

- One sector-specific Pope board.
- Buyer action sheets for the top 3 calls.
- Watchlist CSV with owners, signals, and kill conditions.
- 30-day signal refresh.
- Final retro: what changed, what did not, what decision should update.

## Qualification Questions

- What expensive decision are you making this quarter or this year?
- Which input could delay, strand, or reprice that decision?
- What would you do differently if you had six months earlier warning?
- Who owns the budget if the warning is right?
- What evidence would make you stop believing the warning?
