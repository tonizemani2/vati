# Offer Ladder - The World After AI: intelligence gets permission to act

## Free / Proof

- Public board excerpt with 2 to 3 forecast cards.
- Sealed scorecard entry for each claim.
- Benchmark/social proof shown as credibility, not as the product.

## Design Partner

- One sector-specific Pope board.
- Monthly watchlist refresh.
- Two buyer action sheets tied to active decisions.
- One call with the buyer's team to map decisions, triggers, and kill conditions.
- Target price: low five figures for a short pilot, discounted if they provide feedback and permission to use anonymized proof.

## Pro Subscription

- Quarterly sector boards.
- Alerting on watch signals.
- Scorecard and Brier record.
- Internal memo templates and board-ready slides.
- Target buyer: strategy, investment, procurement, or innovation teams with recurring exposure.

## Enterprise / API

- Custom watchlist integration.
- API summary feed.
- Internal scoring dashboard.
- Dedicated sector coverage and confidential decision memos.
- Target buyer: funds, strategics, insurers, infrastructure investors, and large operators.

## Bespoke Diligence

- One-off constraint audit before an investment, capex, procurement, or partnership decision.
- Price on decision value, not pages produced.
