# Monitoring Runbook - The World After AI: intelligence gets permission to act

## Cadence

- Weekly: check watch signals for top 3 calls.
- Monthly: update watchlist notes and buyer implications.
- Quarterly: publish scorecard status: unchanged, strengthened, weakened, killed, or resolved.

## Status Labels

- `unchanged`: no material signal.
- `strengthened`: watch signal moved toward the thesis.
- `weakened`: kill condition or counter-signal became more plausible.
- `killed`: kill condition occurred.
- `resolved`: dated clause can be scored.

## Calls To Monitor

### P1 - Agent authority rails become the transaction network after AI.

Owner: assign by domain, default to whoever owns agentic commerce / identity / payments coverage.

Primary signal: Watch for two major card networks, processors, or cloud providers reporting production agent credential volume, not just pilots, and for procurement language requiring distinct credentials for autonomous software actors.

Metric: Count of payment, identity, or cloud platforms operating agent credential networks; transaction volume through agent-specific credentials; procurement or regulatory text requiring agent identity and delegated authority for high-value actions.

Kill: Kill if by 2031 agents still transact at scale through ordinary user credentials, API keys, or human-initiated payment flows with no separate agent identity, permission, or liability layer.

Buyer update when signal moves: Payment and identity roadmap priorities shift from human checkout UX toward agent-specific credentials, programmable limits, merchant trust signals, and liability handling.

### P2 - Liability moves to the evidence recorder.

Owner: assign by domain, default to whoever owns liability / insurance / audit evidence coverage.

Primary signal: Watch insurance questionnaires and regulated-sector procurement templates for mandatory AI action-log, incident replay, and retention language.

Metric: Number of G7 or EU-regulated sectors requiring AI action logs or incident records for legal defense, incident reporting, market access, or insurance coverage; policy wording requiring AI event records.

Kill: Kill if by 2031 serious AI incidents are still litigated mainly through ordinary negligence discovery and logs remain voluntary internal telemetry with no sector-specific effect on insurance, legal defense, or market access.

Buyer update when signal moves: AI compliance budgets shift from one-off model evaluations to persistent logging, forensic replay, evidence retention, and independent assurance.

### P3 - Autonomous labs bottleneck on standards before autonomy.

Owner: assign by domain, default to whoever owns autonomous science / lab infrastructure coverage.

Primary signal: Watch DOE, NSF, NIST, pharma, and materials RFPs for named requirements around sample management, instrument communication, data management, and model integration standards.

Metric: Public procurements or funding programs above $25 million requiring named autonomous-lab standards; industrial contracts requiring standards for sample handling, instrument communication, data management, and model integration.

Kill: Kill if by 2031 major autonomous-lab deployments remain dominated by bespoke vendor stacks with no named protocol, sample, data, or instrument standards required in public procurements or industrial contracts.

Buyer update when signal moves: R&D infrastructure budgets shift from isolated robot workcells toward interoperable lab operating systems, data schemas, and integration standards.

### P4 - Materials discovery becomes a qualification-data business.

Owner: assign by domain, default to whoever owns materials / qualification / reliability coverage.

Primary signal: Watch public postmortems of AI-materials programs for phrases like lifetime testing, qualification, design-in delay, field reliability, and accelerated aging.

Metric: Count of AI-discovered or AI-optimized materials missing original commercial design-in targets by at least 36 months due to qualification, reliability, or lifetime evidence; funding or revenue for shared qualification testbeds and data consortia.

Kill: Kill if by 2035 three or more AI-discovered materials reach high-volume design-in within 36 months of disclosure without public qualification delays or shared qualification-data businesses becoming material.

Buyer update when signal moves: R&D budgets shift from model-only discovery tools toward reliability testbeds, reference datasets, and standards participation.

### P5 - Programmable-biology access becomes a licensed screening layer.

Owner: assign by domain, default to whoever owns biosecurity / synthetic biology / access control coverage.

Primary signal: Watch NIH, DOE, EU, OECD, and provider procurement language for requirements around AI-designed sequences, customer verification, record retention, and benchtop synthesis equipment.

Metric: Binding US, EU, or OECD screening rules that go beyond simple sequence-identity matching; number of providers selling AI-resilient screening compliance as a paid API or enterprise product.

Kill: Kill if by 2030 screening remains mostly voluntary and identity-based, with no broader functional or AI-resilient requirement and no paid compliance API market.

Buyer update when signal moves: Biofoundry and synthetic-biology platform budgets shift from raw synthesis speed toward compliance screening, access control, and auditable procurement.

### P6 - Human causal perturbation atlases become the drug-discovery scarce asset.

Owner: assign by domain, default to whoever owns biology / pharma / causal data coverage.

Primary signal: Watch pharma BD language for acquisitions or collaborations where the named asset is a human perturbation atlas, organoid atlas, spatial atlas, or virtual-cell training dataset.

Metric: Top-20 pharma or mega-biotech deals above $500 million that explicitly center proprietary human perturbation, spatial, or organoid atlases; FDA or EMA approval packages citing such platforms as material mechanism or pharmacology evidence.

Kill: Kill if by 2033 fewer than two such major deals are disclosed and approval packages continue relying on conventional animal and in-vitro evidence without atlas-derived human causal evidence.

Buyer update when signal moves: Business-development teams prioritize partnerships and acquisitions around human perturbation atlases rather than model architecture alone.

### P7 - Grid stability replaces megawatts as the AI load gate.

Owner: assign by domain, default to whoever owns energy / data centers / grid stability coverage.

Primary signal: Watch NERC, FERC, ERCOT, PJM, and utility filings for mandatory dynamic models, ride-through rules, or BESS/grid-support requirements for data centers above 100 MW.

Metric: Number of US RTO/ISO regions or NERC/FERC-linked reliability processes requiring large data-center loads to provide dynamic load models and ride-through or grid-support commitments; GW of AI-campus capacity publicly using BESS, grid-forming inverter, or MVDC front ends as interconnection enablers.

Kill: Kill if by 2033 grid operators integrate large AI loads through ordinary substation and capacity studies with no special dynamic-load, EMT-model, ride-through, or grid-support requirements.

Buyer update when signal moves: Data-center capex moves from lowest-cost megawatts toward interconnection-ready power architecture and utility-approved operating behavior.

### P8 - AI campuses become heat-sink and water-rights assets.

Owner: assign by domain, default to whoever owns data-center real estate / cooling / water / heat coverage.

Primary signal: Watch >100 MW AI-campus announcements for WUE targets, reclaimed-water agreements, district-heat offtake, and local moratoria tied to water or heat.

Metric: Number of OECD jurisdictions requiring new large data centers to reuse material waste heat, use non-potable or closed-loop cooling, or prove infeasibility; share of AI-campus announcements citing heat sink, WUE, reclaimed water, or district-heat offtake as a siting reason.

Kill: Kill if by 2032 rules remain disclosure-only and hyperscale AI campuses continue scaling in water- and heat-stressed regions without heat or water constraints materially changing site selection.

Buyer update when signal moves: Site acquisition and power procurement decisions add heat/water rights as first-order diligence, not late-stage sustainability checks.
