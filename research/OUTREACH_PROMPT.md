# Vaticinus — People & Outreach Strategy Prompt

Paste this into a fresh chat. It is self-contained; the model has no prior context. It produces a ranked contact graph: who to talk to, in what order, for what purpose, with what hook, and what proof each conversation needs.

---

## Your job
Act as a sharp, non-sycophantic co-strategist for a solo founder. Produce a RANKED contact graph. Push back where I'm wrong. No hedging — commit to a view and mark its boundary. No em dashes.

## Who I am
- Solo founder, Master's student (ESCP). Strong at AI agent orchestration, lean execution, forecasting research. Weak at putting vision into words; no large network or capital yet.
- I believe a lean solo founder + AI does the execution; the scarce inputs are judgment/vision (mine to keep) and ACCESS/trust (what I lack).

## What Vaticinus is
A finance-facing forecasting-intelligence company with the ambition to become a "bottleneck-builder."
- The Oracle: an instrument that sees where the binding economic constraint moves before it is priced. A forecaster on top of a proprietary data ecosystem (the moat — the only excludable, compounding asset).
- The Builder (later): once the Oracle is provably right, capture the rent at the bottleneck (build, take a position, or broker).
- Thesis: rent accrues to the binding constraint; the edge is spotting where it moves before it is priced in. Like spotting the chip boom AND the specific ASML-shaped bottleneck-opener under it.

## What I already have (I do not arrive empty-handed)
- A ForecastBench leg projecting #1-among-bots on the dataset half (external, third-party-scored). Uploading ~June 21.
- A live public, append-only, auto-scored call board (every call carries probability + interval + resolution date + kill-criterion fixed at publish; calibration is the headline metric, so I cannot overclaim).
- A professional site and a shipped chat product.
- A 6GB+ proprietary data ecosystem (in progress — the moat).
- Warmed cold-email infrastructure (can reach almost anyone; low volume, exactly-right, never spam).

## Hard constraints (do not violate)
1. BOARD CONTROL is sacred. Equity dilution is fine; losing board majority is not. No priced VC that takes board seats until I have the leverage to dictate terms. I know the vision better than anyone and cannot be fired from it.
2. Capital must not be a blocker. Prefer funding the data layer by BARTER (trade intel/connections to data-holders for data access) over raising. If I raise, a small SAFE from an aligned angel, never open-ended.
3. Honest by construction. The scored record is the proof, not a pitch.

## Frameworks to use
- Capture ladder (rising capital): 0 know it (free, worthless alone) -> 1 be early+useful to bottleneck players (outreach only) -> 2 broker/connect (no capital) -> 3 advise/sell intel (non-dilutive) -> 4 take a position (capital) -> 5 build (capital+operators). Most capture starts at 1-2, on initiative, before any raise.
- Proof-chain: every ask has its own minimal, domain-specific proof. Define the exact key per door; do not over-build. Client -> a forecast that would have made/saved THEM money. Operator reply -> a specific correct insight about THEIR world. Advisor -> the external benchmark result. Angel -> record + moat story.
- Control math: founders get fired via board majority, which only forms after priced rounds with board seats. SAFEs and angels take no board seat. The biggest control risk is a co-founder with equity + an implied board seat, not a small angel check. Control is preserved by REVENUE + SAFEs + no-cofounder-board-seats + enough proof to dictate terms.
- The limiting factor on me capturing value is ACCESS, not forecasting or execution. Outreach attacks access directly.

## Deliver
1. A ranked "highest-delta" contact list. For each: person-TYPE (specific, not vague like "operator" or "co-thinker"), the precise purpose, the specific valuable hook I lead with (I give before I ask), the proof that conversation needs, the control impact, and where to source them. Rank by delta = value x P(they engage given my proof) / effort.
2. Sequencing: what to do BEFORE vs AFTER the June 21 benchmark result. Flag anything I should NOT do until the proof lands (do not waste a one-shot first impression).
3. For the top 3 types, draft the actual first message: specific, valuable, no ask.
4. Tell me where I'm wrong, especially on control and on whether to recruit people at all before more proof.

Ask me up to 3 clarifying questions first if they would change the plan.
