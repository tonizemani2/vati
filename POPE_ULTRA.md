# Pope Ultra

Pope Mega answers: **what future constraint is likely to matter?**

Pope Ultra answers: **who exactly should do what, with which permits, assets,
organizations, people, budget lines, verification links, and stop conditions?**

It is the layer between a forecast board and an operating action.

## The Product Shift

Do not sell "prediction." Sell a verified operating dossier for an exposed
decision.

Every Ultra dossier starts from one Pope thesis and compiles it into:

1. The scored claim: dated clause, probability, metric, kill condition.
2. The exposed decision: capex, procurement, siting, research, hiring, policy,
   portfolio, partnership, or founding wedge.
3. The real-world map: permits, dockets, projects, suppliers, labs, universities,
   people, buyers, contracts, watch signals, and contact paths.
4. The money/action plan: what amount, into which operation, why now, what
   milestone unlocks the next tranche, what kills the action.
5. The verification ledger: every named fact has a source tier, source URL,
   retrieval date, quote/span, confidence, and status.

## The Truth Rule

Pope Ultra is allowed to be aggressive in search, but conservative in storage.

No named permit, company, person, contact, project, lab, or dollar amount is
decision-grade until it is source-verified.

Statuses:

- `unverified`: generated by reasoning or search query planning only.
- `lead`: discovered by search or secondary mention, not yet checked.
- `source_verified`: supported by a credible source.
- `primary_verified`: supported by a primary source such as a permit docket,
  regulator, official company page, filing, grant record, procurement portal,
  university lab page, or standards body.
- `contact_confirmed`: public business contact route verified on an official
  page or filing.
- `refuted`: source check contradicted the candidate.

Contacts are never guessed. Use official business contact routes, filings,
procurement portals, public university directories, company leadership pages,
or explicit public emails. Do not infer email patterns.

## The Layers

### L0 - Claim Integrity

Preserve the original Pope thesis fields. Ultra may translate but may not
strengthen the scored claim, probability, resolution, metric, or kill condition.

### L1 - Decision Surface

Name the decision that changes. Examples:

- Put this much money into a site option or project diligence.
- Pull forward a procurement contract before lead times widen.
- Hire or train a specific skill before the labor market tightens.
- Fund a university lab or research method because it becomes the bottleneck.
- Enter a market, acquire a supplier, or form a partnership.
- Avoid a bad capex plan because the supposed asset cannot clear permits.

### L2 - Named World Map

Turn the thesis into a hunt for named objects:

- Permits and dockets.
- Project sites and assets.
- Companies, suppliers, buyers, and developers.
- Universities, labs, principal investigators, research centers.
- Policy bodies, standards bodies, regulators, agencies.
- People with operational authority or unusually relevant expertise.
- Public business contact paths.

### L3 - Verification

Each named object must have source requirements before it is trusted:

- Source tier: primary, official, credible secondary, or lead only.
- URL, date retrieved, source owner.
- Verbatim span or table row.
- Field extracted: permit name, docket id, applicant, jurisdiction, status,
  capacity, deadline, budget, owner, contact route, etc.
- Open question and next check.

### L4 - Action Economics

Convert the verified map into a trancheable action:

- Amount range.
- Operation funded.
- Why this creates ROI or avoids loss.
- Trigger for first tranche.
- Trigger for next tranche.
- Kill condition.
- Downside and reversibility.
- Owner and date.

### L5 - Outreach

Produce a short contact plan:

- Who to contact.
- Why them.
- What to ask.
- What proof to attach.
- What answer would change the forecast or action.
- Public contact path only.

### L6 - Monitoring

Every action gets watch signals and stop conditions. The dossier is not done
when written; it is done when it can be checked on a cadence.

## CLI

Generate Ultra scaffolds from a Pope JSON:

```bash
python3 -m engine.pope.ultra research/pope/after-ai-2026-06-17.json \
  --out-dir research/pope/after-ai-2026-06-17.ultra
```

Generate only one thesis:

```bash
python3 -m engine.pope.ultra research/pope/after-ai-2026-06-17.json \
  --thesis P1 \
  --out-dir research/pope/after-ai-2026-06-17.ultra
```

Outputs:

- `ultra.json`: source-of-truth structured dossier.
- `ultra.md`: human-readable operating dossier.
- `task_queue.csv`: flattened research and verification tasks.
- `contacts_seed.csv`: contact-path tasks, not guessed contact data.

This module intentionally emits unknowns as tasks. The next agentic layer can
search/fetch/extract those tasks, but should only promote them when the truth
rule is satisfied.
