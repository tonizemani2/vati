# /wildcard — diverge wide (the front door the gate can't see)

The repo is a machine for **converging** (gate, saturation eye, skeptic, kill-criteria, Brier — disciplined disposal). But disposal can only filter what generation surfaced. `/needle` and `/gate` are *convergent* tools — they hunt binding **constraints** in the house template. This one is the opposite phase: **generate IDEAS wide, with the aperture fully open**, so the creative misses (the deep-learning-class blind spot, the force we never modelled) get a chance to be born before any filter runs. Read `doctrine.md §0.7` first — it's the canon for this lane. $0/keyless; log before any spend.

## The trap this exists to kill
The system preaches *fox* (§1, many lenses) but is built as a *hedgehog* — every collector, funnel and template bets on one thesis: **rent → binding constraint → one layer deeper.** Anything not shaped like that dies at *generation*, never reaching a gate. `/wildcard` reopens generation. **A constraint-shaped candidate is NOT the only acceptable output here** — that's `/needle`'s job.

## What's legitimate here (and punished nowhere)
- **Cross-domain analogy** — a pattern from field A landing in B before anyone's named it.
- **Category obsolescence** — the thing that makes the constraint *moot* before it binds (the substitution, the routing-around, the demand that evaporates).
- **Emergent behaviour & second-order / social cascades** — a new behaviour, a reflexive loop, a political/demographic shift that reorganizes value.
- **Two-trend collisions** — where two curves intersect into something neither predicts alone.
- **Inversions of our own house thesis** — *what if rent does NOT migrate to the constraint here — what eats it instead?*
- **Expert disagreement as raw material** — hunt where smart people *conflict* and what they *all* assume unexamined. (Never anchor to the median take — that's the contamination rule, and it binds on the convergent side, not here. `doctrine.md §0, §4, §0.7`.)

Quantity over polish. A half-formed idea is welcome. **No idea is killed at birth for not yet being a forecast.**

## Procedure
1. **Pick a provocation** (the `$ARGUMENTS`, or roam free): a domain, a tension, a "what's everyone missing," or nothing — open scan.
2. **Generate wide.** Produce many candidate ideas across the shapes above. Lean into the un-house-thesis ones — that's the whole point. Don't self-censor for falsifiability yet.
3. **Cluster & mark the live ones.** Group them; flag the few that feel real or generative. For each live one, name in one line *why it might matter* and *what phase it's in* (raw idea / could-graduate / needs-a-mechanism).
4. **Hand the graduation candidates to the convergent lane — do NOT score them here.** An idea that looks real goes to `/needle` → `/gate` → `hypothesis-add` to be reshaped into a falsifiable, dated claim and forced through the full gate. Until then it is *only an idea*: un-scored, outside the registry, polluting nothing.

## Output contract — what you hand back
A wide list, then a short tail of live ones. For each **live** idea, ≤4 lines:
- **The idea:** stated plainly (any shape — not forced into constraint-form).
- **Why it might matter:** the non-obvious bet / what consensus isn't seeing (1 line).
- **Shape:** which kind (analogy / obsolescence / emergence / collision / house-thesis inversion).
- **Graduation path:** what it would take to make it falsifiable + dated → hand to `/needle`/`/gate`, OR honestly "parks (no testable series yet)".

## Hard rules
- **Generate freely, but graduate strictly.** Nothing enters the immutable record without clearing the full convergent gate (the anti-astrology valve holds absolutely — `doctrine.md §0.7`).
- **Do NOT** create a Hypothesis, ForecastCard, or bet here, and **do NOT** run the priced-in gate — this lane only *opens* the aperture. Convergence + Ruben's nod (rule 7) happen downstream.
- A park is not a failure — an un-falsifiable-yet idea is logged as a prompt to find its core, never discarded for not fitting the mold.
- If the scan is dry, say so plainly. But dryness here usually means the provocation was too narrow — widen it.
