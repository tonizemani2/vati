# Reference: voice, channels, formulas, scoring — loaded on demand by SKILL.md

This is the "what good looks like" bank for turning Pope calls (and the scored record) into
content. The source material is rich: every call already has a plain-English mechanism (`boom`),
named winners/losers, a "what's not priced" angle (`pre_consensus`), a live price anchor
(`price_channel`), a dated monitorable (`watch`), and a disconfirmation (`kill`). Your job is
selection and compression, not invention.

The brand sells one thing: **honest, leak-free, falsifiable foresight.** Slop copy or an
overclaimed track record undercuts the only asset we have. Every rule below protects that.

---

## House voice (follow exactly)

```
You write for sharp generalists, allocators, operators, and journalists who like pre-consensus
market writing. Voice is an institutional research note written by a human who has conviction
and shows their work.

- Lead with the call, not the setup. Line 1 names the surprising constraint or the dated number.
  No "In a world where...", no "Let's talk about...", no throat-clearing.
- Short sentences. Plain words. One idea per paragraph.
- State the mechanism in cause-and-effect, not adjectives. "X is byproduct-locked, so supply
  cannot answer price" beats "X faces unprecedented supply challenges."
- Always show the boundary of the claim. A forecast without a kill criterion is a horoscope.
  Saying how you would be proven wrong is the credential, not a weakness.
- Two probabilities when the call has them: the direction (vision) and the strict dated clause.
  The coin-flip look of a strict clause is honesty, not timidity. Say so plainly.
- Name who gets richer and who gets hurt, but never tell anyone to buy or sell. We map rent
  migration. We do not give investment advice. "The rent lands on X" not "buy X."
- If a fact is time-sensitive (a price, an export ban, a closure), it was true when the call was
  authored, not necessarily today. Re-check it live or frame it as "as of <date>."
```

### The no-AI-slop rules (hard — Ruben flags these)
- **No em-dashes. Ever.** Use a comma, a colon, a period, or parentheses.
- No rule-of-three padding ("faster, cheaper, and smarter").
- No AI vocabulary: delve, tapestry, testament, landscape, realm, underscore, pivotal, crucial,
  navigate the complexities, game-changer, in today's fast-paced.
- No negative parallelism ("It's not just X, it's Y") as a crutch. Use it at most once, earned.
- No "the real question is", "importantly", "notably", "it's worth noting".
- No hype adjectives on our own work (groundbreaking, revolutionary). Let the call carry it.
- Every draft ends by going through the `humanizer` skill (or its checklist inline). No exceptions.

## Post formulas (pick one per piece; fill every input from the postpack card)

1. **The named needle** — "Everyone watches X. The ceiling is actually Y, and Y is [one site /
   one byproduct / one trained cohort]." inputs: consensus_target, the_needle, why_inelastic,
   rent_winner. (Best for: chips Zeiss, IMS, hafnium, iridium.)
2. **The dated reversal** — "Consensus says X. We think the opposite by <date>, because the fix
   is already paid for: <named supply step>." inputs: consensus, counter_call, dated_catalyst,
   resolve_date, kill. (Best for: the near-term commodity calls.)
3. **The hidden squeeze** — "The headline number (LME nickel, 'nickel surplus') hides the one
   that matters (battery-grade sulfate)." inputs: headline_number, real_number, decoupling_reason,
   magnitude, who_eats_the_cost.
4. **Rent migration map** — "If this call is right, here is exactly what moves." inputs: call,
   rent_path, winners (named), losers (named), what_reprices, next_constraint.
5. **The scoreboard** — a thread of 3-5 dated calls with their resolve dates and kill lines, framed
   as "score us in 18 months." inputs: 3-5 NEAR calls, each with resolve_date + kill. (This is the
   public-track-record builder. Use it sparingly and let it stand on the dates.)
6. **Two-probability honesty** — "We are 82% sure of the direction and 52% sure of the exact dated
   clause. Here is why the gap exists." inputs: vision_p, clause_p, the_strict_clause, the_gap_reason.
7. **The biology wall** — for constraints capital cannot fund (donors, organs, decade-trained
   people). inputs: the_input, why_unfakeable, the_demographic_clock, the_gated_escape.
8. **The physics wall** — for constraints cheap capex cannot move (radiators, lunar night, byproduct
   ratios). inputs: the_law, the_floor_it_sets, why_no_learning_curve, who_owns_the_workaround.
9. **What the market hasn't priced** — lead with the live anchor, then the unpriced residual.
   inputs: live_price, whats_priced, whats_not_priced, the_instrument_that_would_move.
10. **The contrarian kill** — publish the call AND the single thing that would prove us wrong, and
    invite the reader to watch that marker. inputs: call, kill_marker, resolve_date.

Lean on 1, 2, 3, 4, 9 most. Use 5 and 6 to build the honesty brand. 7 and 8 are the durable
"framework" pieces a generalist screenshots.

## Hook patterns (line 1)

- The reframe: "Everyone pricing the AI chip is watching the wrong factory."
- The dated counter: "Consensus says memory stays expensive. We think DDR5 falls 35% by end-2027."
- The one fact: "Over 90% of the world's hafnium is a leftover. You cannot make more without
  overproducing nuclear reactor cladding nobody ordered."
- The named site: "The ceiling on the entire AI wafer build-out is one polishing floor in
  Oberkochen, Germany, running at about one mirror set a year."
- The honest odds: "We will publicly take both sides of our own confidence on this one."

Avoid: question hooks ("Is hafnium the new lithium?"), webinar-title energy, and any hook that
front-loads Vaticinus instead of the call.

## Scoring rubric (run on every draft before humanizing; ship at >= 80)

Score each 0-10, weight, total /100. Be a ruthless editor who hates filler and overclaiming.

1. **Hook & stop-power** (x1.5) — does line 1 earn the click with a specific surprise?
2. **Insight & non-obviousness** (x2.5) — does a smart reader learn the needle, or is it a restated
   headline? The whole product is variant perception. Reward it.
3. **Mechanism clarity** (x2.0) — is the cause-and-effect legible to a non-expert? Jargon decoded?
4. **Integrity & framing** (x2.0) — dated, falsifiable, kill line present, no track-record overclaim,
   no investment advice, time-sensitive facts flagged. **A missing kill line, an implied track
   record we don't have, or a buy/sell instruction caps the total at 55.**
5. **Voice** (x1.0) — tight, human, conviction-led, zero AI tells, no em-dashes.
6. **Platform fit** (x1.0) — right length and shape for the channel, hook lands before the fold.

Verdict: SHIP (>= 80) / REVISE (60-79) / KILL (< 60). If REVISE, name the 2-3 edits that move it most.

## The Claim-Integrity Ledger (ships with every piece, like mining-post's verify ledger)

A non-expert owner has to be able to post this safely. So every piece ends with a short ledger:

```
— CLAIM-INTEGRITY CHECK (clear before posting) —
• Source call: <file>.json id=<id>  (hardened candidate, authored <date>, NOT forward-tracked)
• Dated claim present? yes  → resolves <date>
• Kill line present in the post? yes  → "<the kill, one line>"
• Track-record framing? we present this as a CALL, not a past win. (confirm no "we predicted X")
• Investment advice? none. We map rent; we do not say buy/sell. (confirm)
• Time-sensitive facts to re-verify LIVE before posting (the JSON froze on its authored date):
    - <each price / export ban / closure / "as of" fact> → re-check: <how, in 60s>
• Numbers used → all trace to the postpack card (no figure typed from memory). yes/no
```

Rule: any time-sensitive fact (a spot price, an export ban, a strait closure, a "first deficit
since" claim) is re-checked with WebSearch before the piece ships, or reframed as "as of <author
date>." The Pope JSON is a snapshot, not live. This is the single biggest brand risk for a
near-term call.
